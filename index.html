Simple ways to protect your plumbing

A little care goes a long way. Avoid pouring grease down the kitchen drain, and use strainers in showers and tubs to catch hair. Learn where your main water shutoff valve is so you can act quickly during a leak. Flush your water heater once a year to reduce sediment, and check under sinks now and then for dampness or small drips.

Watch your water bill too. A sudden increase with no change in habits can point to a hidden leak. Our leak detection and repair service can locate the source before it turns into mold, stained ceilings or damaged flooring. Catching problems early is almost always cheaper than waiting.

What to do in a plumbing emergency

If a pipe bursts or a fixture overflows, stay calm and shut off the water at the main valve, which is usually near the water meter or where the main line enters the house. Turn off the power to any area where water is near outlets or appliances. Then call our emergency plumber High Point team so we can start helping right away.

If you smell gas, do not use switches, flames or phones inside. Leave the home, move to a safe distance and call the gas utility and emergency services. After that, contact us for any repair or gas line work that follows.

For a water heater leak, turn off the heater and close the cold water supply valve on top of the tank. For a clogged toilet that will not stop rising, lift the tank lid and close the flapper or the supply valve. These small steps can protect your floors until we arrive.
